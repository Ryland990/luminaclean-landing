# Banned words & patterns — FolioKit content rule

Owner-approved 2026-07-15. EVERY piece of public writing (blog posts, docs,
site copy, launch/social posts) must pass this list before publishing. Agents:
check your draft against every section; when a banned word is load-bearing,
rewrite the sentence — don't synonym-swap.

## Filler intensifiers (ban outright)
actually · honestly · truly · really · simply · just (as filler) · genuinely ·
literally · frankly · seamlessly · effortlessly

## Marketing-slop verbs & buzzwords
leverage · unlock · unleash · supercharge · elevate · empower · streamline ·
transform · revolutionize · game-changer · robust · comprehensive ·
cutting-edge · blazing-fast · world-class · seamless · effortless · delve ·
deep dive · navigate (metaphorical) · journey · landscape · crucial · vital

## AI sentence patterns
- "It's not just X — it's Y"
- "Here's the thing:" / "Let's face it" / "The best part?" / "Sound familiar?"
- Quotable antithesis lines ("X is the whole landscape; the rest is detail")
- Triple-punch fragments ("Fast. Simple. Powerful.")
- "In today's fast-paced world…" openers
- "It's worth noting" / "Importantly" / "Interestingly" / "Needless to say"
- Rhetorical questions as section headers

## Punctuation
- Em-dashes (—): maximum ONE per paragraph
- No "X: Why Y Matters"-pattern colon titles
- No arrows (→) in prose
- No emoji in headings or article body text

## Replacement guidance
Prefer the concrete specific over the intensifier ("saves 40 minutes" not
"really fast"), the literal over the metaphor, and one plain sentence over a
quotable one. If a sentence sounds like it wants to be screenshot-quoted,
rewrite it until it doesn't.
